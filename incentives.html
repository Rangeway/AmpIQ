How incentives
actually work.

Charging projects may qualify for support from federal, state, and utility programs. The available combination depends on the property, timing, and current program rules.

See what you qualify forSee the three layers

The three-layer stack

Three programs, one project.

Each layer is a separate program with its own rules, paperwork, and timing. They can apply to the same project at the same time.

01

Federal

National programs that can apply to qualifying charging infrastructure. This layer is often the most powerful, and the most time-sensitive, because it shifts with federal tax law.

02

State

State-level grants and rebates vary widely depending on where your property sits. Two identical projects in different states can qualify for very different support.

03

Utility

Your local utility is often the most overlooked layer of all. Many utilities offer make-ready programs, rebates, or rate structures that are rarely advertised.

All incentive guidance is qualitative and based on current program availability. Specific programs, eligibility, and the federal layer in particular change over time, so we verify what applies to your project before it shapes any decision.
