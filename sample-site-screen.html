Illustrative retail parcel
Use case
Public-facing DC fast charging
Decision date
Sample only

Recommendation

Advance conditionally.

The parcel appears to merit deeper diligence, provided the owner confirms service capacity and an easement condition before treating the project as viable.

What appears favorable

  • Visible parking geometry appears capable of supporting a small initial charging deployment without changing primary circulation.
  • The intended use appears generally compatible with the surrounding commercial context, subject to local confirmation.
  • The property’s access and dwell-time profile support a deeper utilization and site-design review.

What could change the answer

  • Available utility capacity and the cost or timing of any required service upgrade are not confirmed.
  • A recorded access or utility easement may constrain the preferred equipment location.
  • Local development standards and the applicable permitting path require confirmation with the governing jurisdiction.

Recommended next diligence

Prove the material unknowns in the right order.

01

Utility inquiry

Confirm service configuration, available capacity, upgrade pathway, and likely schedule with the serving utility.

02

Site assessment

Validate electrical conditions, parking flow, trenching route, charger locations, and civil constraints in the field.

03

Jurisdiction check

Confirm entitlement, zoning, accessibility, and permitting requirements before finalizing the project design.

04

Engineering scope

Commission project-specific design only after the site clears the questions that matter most.

This fictional memo is illustrative only. A real screen is site-specific, based on available information at the time of review, and does not replace utility confirmation, engineering, legal review, or permitting.
