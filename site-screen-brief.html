We examine

The questions worth asking before technical work begins.

  • Serving utility and publicly available service context.
  • Parcel access, visible easement considerations, and parking layout.
  • Readily identifiable zoning, use, setback, and permitting considerations.
  • Site-fit observations, material unknowns, and the next diligence steps.

We deliver

A clear recommendation, not manufactured certainty.

  • Advance, advance conditionally, or stop.
  • The reasoning behind the recommendation in plain language.
  • The facts that need utility, field, engineering, legal, or jurisdictional confirmation.
  • A right-sized plan for deeper diligence if the site advances.

Important scope boundary

This is not engineering in disguise.

The Site Screen does not include a field visit, electrical study, stamped design, utility capacity confirmation, interconnection approval, permit approval, incentive commitment, final project budget, or forecast. It helps determine whether those investments are justified.
