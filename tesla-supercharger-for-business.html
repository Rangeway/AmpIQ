Field guide · Tesla Supercharger for Business

An independent
second opinion.

Tesla Supercharger for Business lets a commercial property owner purchase Superchargers, set charging prices, and use Tesla's integrated network operations and service. It can be a strong opportunity, or the wrong fit for your site. We tell you which, before you commit.

Get an honest assessmentHow it works

What you get

Ownership and revenue control.

You purchase and own the Superchargers installed at your property, set session pricing, and track financials through Tesla's portal. Tesla provides the hardware, software, commissioning, network operations, preventative maintenance, and driver support.

What Tesla leaves to you

Everything before the chargers turn on.

Installation, permitting, utility interconnection, and capturing the incentives your project may qualify for all stay on your plate. Tesla provides project engineering support, but your site still needs a plan that reflects your property and operating goals.

The honest second opinion

We run the numbers without a sales agenda.

Tesla's estimate is a useful starting point, but it is not a site-specific underwriting conclusion. Your traffic, site control, utility capacity, installation cost, and incentive eligibility all shape what the opportunity actually looks like.

We start where the estimate stops. If Tesla is the right call, we coordinate everything the program leaves to you. If it is not, we show you hardware-agnostic alternatives worth comparing.

Often a fit

Your site already pulls the right traffic.

Properties on travel corridors, near strong demand, or with the dwell time and visibility that drive charging sessions. Sites where the four-charger minimum is a floor, not a stretch.

Rarely a fit

Your site is quiet, remote, or already amenitized.

Properties with low visitor turnover, limited utility capacity, or where guest charging is a service, not a revenue center. Level 2 or a smaller DC deployment may better fit the site.
