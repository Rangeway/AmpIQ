A better first answer
for the charging question.

When a buyer, tenant, or owner asks whether a property can support EV charging, AmpIQ can give them a clear desk-based first screen before they incur engineering or construction costs.

UsePre-acquisition or lease
FormatDecision memo
Timing7 to 10 business days
OutcomeA clearer next step

01

When to refer

Refer a client when EV charging affects the property decision, the deal has a diligence window, or the client needs to know whether a site deserves more technical work.

02

What the client gets

A concise recommendation to advance, advance conditionally, or stop, with the visible constraints, material unknowns, and the right next steps.

03

What it does not do

It does not substitute for a field visit, engineering, utility capacity confirmation, land-use advice, or permitting. It helps the client deploy each of those resources intentionally.

A simple introduction

“Before you spend on engineering or commit to the property based on assumptions, I’d suggest a Pre-Acquisition Site Screen with AmpIQ. They will give you a desk-based first read on the charging opportunity, the likely constraints, and what would need to be confirmed next.”
